# Training plans and sessions

The Plans page is for the training that leaves no GPS trace: gym work, written
down once and then run week after week.

## A plan

A plan is a set of **days** — Push, Pull, Legs — and each day holds **blocks**.
A block is usually one exercise, but it can be:

- a **superset** or a choice of alternatives, when it holds more than one
  exercise, with an optional wait between them;
- a **section** — a warm-up, a cool-down, a stretch — which can be nothing but
  a length of time.

An exercise carries sets, and then either reps and a weight, or a duration.
Bodyweight exercises take *added* weight rather than total. A rest can be set
after any exercise and after any block.

Opening a plan shows the day you are about to train, laid out to be read.
Editing is a mode you ask for, from the plan's ⋮ menu.

## Running a session

**Start** — from the plan, or straight from a row in the plans list — begins a
session. The runner shows the exercise you are on, what is coming, and one
timer. Finishing a set ticks it off; finishing the last set of an exercise moves
you on and starts the rest, with the break named above the countdown so a rest
between sets and a break between blocks tell themselves apart.

The bar along the top carries the time now, the time elapsed, and how far
through the session you are. A session survives a reload, a phone lock, and
being opened on another device — it lives on the server, not in the tab.

You can stop a session (it stays in history, unfinished) or discard it.

### Signals

Under **Settings → Plans → Signals**, a session can announce itself with a
**vibration**, a **sound**, or both, at the moments your hands are busy: a set
ticked, an exercise finished, a rest ending, the session starting and ending.
**Announce a rest from** sets how long a rest has to be before it gets its own
signal — shorter ones you spend watching the clock anyway.

These are kept per device, not on your account, because a phone in the gym and
a laptop at home want different answers.

### Sessions as workouts

**Settings → Plans → Finished sessions** can record every finished session as a
workout, so gym work counts towards your streaks, goals and totals. Either way
the session stays in the plan's history with its sets, weights and duration.

## History

The **History** tab lists every session you have run — as rows or as cards, and
filterable by plan, date, status and length. Opening one shows what you
actually did, next to what the plan asked for.

Each plan and each session has its own **notes**, private to you, and — once
shared — a **discussion** beside them.

## Sharing a plan

A plan or a finished session can be made public to everyone on the server, or
shared with specific people, from its ⋮ menu. See
[Sharing and people](sharing.md); the short version is that someone you share a
plan with can **clone** it into their own library and edit their copy, and that
your notes are never part of what they see.

## Where it shows up elsewhere

The Consistency page's **Training Sessions** chart counts finished sessions by
the week they started. Each bar is a week's sets, split into one band per
session — and weeks you did not train are drawn empty rather than skipped.
